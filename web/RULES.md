# Rules of Creating a Domain/Extension

If either of these are broken without reading, this will result in a deletion on either, your website, or your domain extension. which will be applied before addition.

## Rules

* **Extensions:**

  > Extensions are 2 to 4 characters long. Examples include `.com`, `.net`, `.site`, or `.ai`, chosen based on your website's focus. For instance, AI platforms use `.ai` (such as [Stability.ai](https://stability.ai/), creator of Stable Diffusion, or [Claude.ai](https://claude.ai/), the official site for Claude AI), while commercial projects commonly use `.com` (such as [Google](https://google.com/)).

* **Domains:**

  > Domains are essential internet identifiers. Without them, navigating the web would be nearly impossible. Keep these core rules in mind when defining and registering your domain:
  >
  > 1. **Uniqueness:** Every domain must be distinct to prevent routing collisions across the network.
  > 2. **Structure:** Follow the standard hierarchical format (e.g., `subdomain.domain.extension`), or use `domain.extension` for more easy domain handling.
  > 3. **Length Restrictions:** Keep domain names concise, typically between 3 and 63 characters long for readability.
  > 4. **Character Set:** Use only allowed characters, primarily lowercase alphanumeric characters and hyphens (avoiding leading or trailing hyphens). Profanity and sensitive strings are generally permitted by standard registries (mindful of the "Scunthorpe Problem" and platform-specific advertising policies like Google Ads), but must still adhere to community standards.
  > 5. **Extension Matching:** Pair your domain with an appropriate extension (`.nw`, `.it.now`, etc.) that matches the project's ecosystem guidelines.
  > 6. **Historical Tracking:** Maintain a clean log of previously registered domains to prevent dead links and manage deprecations.
  > 7. **Namespace Reservation:** Avoid using reserved or restricted system keywords that could conflict with core routing.
  > 8. **Resolution Accuracy:** Ensure every active domain points correctly to its designated NiceGUI application route or asset.
  > 9. **Documentation Link:** Reference the official `DOMAINS.md` file whenever registering or modifying custom domains, or clone this repository to register a new domain, like myrelativedog on the examples folder.
  > 10. **Lifecycle Management:** Clean up or archive expired domain records to keep the tracking schema lightweight and updated.
